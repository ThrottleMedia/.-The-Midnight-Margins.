Poetry for the
beautifully restless soul.

For the thoughts that arrive after midnight. For the songs that understand us before people do. For every heart that has ever felt like a library with its lights still on.

A NOTE IN THE MARGIN

Some things are too large to keep inside us.
So we give them words.

Read slowly. Stay a little. Take what finds you. Leave the rest for another night.

THE SOUNDTRACK

Music for the unsaid

Some songs are rooms we can return to. Follow a melody into a memory. Let an old song introduce you to a feeling you did not know had a name.

THE MARGINALIA

Notes from a curious mind

Collect beautiful words, underline strange sentences, argue with the narrator, keep a list of questions. Literature is a conversation that keeps finding new voices.

THE WILD THOUGHT

A little rebellion

Stay curious in a world that rewards certainty. Be earnest. Notice the moon on an ordinary street. Refuse to become too busy to be amazed.
